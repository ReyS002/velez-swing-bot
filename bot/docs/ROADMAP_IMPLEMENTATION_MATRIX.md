## Audit conclusions

- Existing strategy, scanner, webhook interpretation, broker routing, and execution gates are out of scope and must remain unchanged.
- The new planner is advisory and must call the existing risk calculations; it cannot stage or submit an order.
- Existing top-down analysis is the market-context authority. New readiness and discipline scores consume its output without becoming execution gates.
- Two additive tables are justified: structured trade reviews and private symbol thesis notes. They require no destructive migration and older databases remain valid.
- TradingView remains cross-origin. Verified levels will be presented in a synchronized adjacent layer and as TradingView links, never by manipulating the iframe.
