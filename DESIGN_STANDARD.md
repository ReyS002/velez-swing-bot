# Trading Desk visual standard

User-approved reference: “Codex Image Sep 10, 2026, 10_54_12 PM.png”, supplied September 10, 2026. A preserved copy is at the rollout workspace's design-reference/approved-visual-quality.png.

Future room renders and implemented objects must match its photoreal quality: physically convincing brushed brass and dark enamel, substantial beveled edges, realistic glass reflections, warm shelf lighting, accurate perspective, contact shadows and restrained scale. Flat CSS illustrations are not an acceptable substitute for the approved rendered object. Judge quality in the full room as well as a close-up; render approval alone does not prove the implementation matches it.

Tokyo, Dubai, Hawaii and New York should have no added session-clock object or regional decorative accents (exchange bell, koa box/lava stone, astrolabe or Fuji ornament). Preserve the existing functional safe, journal, phone, music player, bull/bear statue and room scenery. Executive’s added clock and architectural miniature are also removed. The gauge remains removed everywhere.
